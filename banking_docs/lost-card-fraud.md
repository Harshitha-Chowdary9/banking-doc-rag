# Meridian Bank - Lost Cards and Fraud Reporting (FICTIONAL)

Meridian Bank is a fictional institution created for this study project.

If a debit card is lost or stolen, the customer should freeze it immediately
in the app or call the 24/7 hotline. A replacement card arrives within 3-5
business days; express delivery within 48 hours costs 10 EUR. Unauthorised
card transactions must be reported within 13 months, but reporting promptly
protects the customer: under the fictional Meridian guarantee, customers are
not liable for unauthorised transactions reported after the card is frozen,
and liability before reporting is capped at 50 EUR except in cases of gross
negligence such as sharing a PIN. Disputed transactions are provisionally
refunded within 10 business days while investigated.
