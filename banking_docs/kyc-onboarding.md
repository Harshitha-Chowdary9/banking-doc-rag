# Meridian Bank - Account Opening and KYC (FICTIONAL)

Meridian Bank is a fictional institution created for this study project.

Opening an account takes about 10 minutes in the app. Know Your Customer
(KYC) rules require a valid passport or national ID card and a short video
selfie for identity verification. Proof of address (utility bill or bank
statement dated within 3 months) is required for customers resident outside
the issuing country of their ID. Accounts are usually active within one
business day after successful verification. Applications may be referred for
manual review, which takes up to 3 business days. Customers must be 18 or
older and resident in a supported country.
