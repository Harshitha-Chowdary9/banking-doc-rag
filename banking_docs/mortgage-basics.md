# Meridian Bank - Mortgage Basics (FICTIONAL)

Meridian Bank is a fictional institution created for this study project.

Meridian offers fixed-rate mortgages with terms of 5, 10, or 15 years and
loan-to-value ratios up to 80%. The fictional indicative rate for a 10-year
fix at 60% LTV is 3.45% APR. Early repayment of up to 10% of the outstanding
balance per year is free; larger early repayments carry a 1% fee on the
excess. Applications require proof of income for the last 3 months, ID, and
details of existing commitments. A mortgage in principle decision is given
within 48 hours. Monthly payments are collected by SEPA direct debit on the
1st of each month.
