# Meridian Bank - SEPA Transfers FAQ (FICTIONAL)

Meridian Bank is a fictional institution created for this study project.

SEPA credit transfers in euros are free and unlimited on the Everyday
Current Account. Transfers submitted before 15:00 CET on a business day are
processed the same day and usually arrive the next business day; transfers
submitted later are processed the next business day. SEPA instant payments
are supported up to 15,000 EUR per transaction and arrive within seconds,
24/7, for a 0.50 EUR fee. The standard transfer limit is 25,000 EUR per day,
adjustable in the app between 500 and 25,000 EUR. International (non-SEPA)
transfers carry a 12.50 EUR fee and may use correspondent banks that deduct
their own charges.
