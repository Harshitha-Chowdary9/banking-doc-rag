# Meridian Bank - Everyday Current Account Terms (FICTIONAL)

Meridian Bank is a fictional institution created for this study project.

The Everyday Current Account has no monthly fee when at least 1,000 EUR is
deposited per calendar month; otherwise a 4.95 EUR monthly maintenance fee
applies. The account includes a contactless debit card, SEPA credit
transfers, and up to 5 free cash withdrawals per month at partner ATMs.
Additional withdrawals cost 1.50 EUR each. Overdrafts are not automatic; see
the overdraft policy. Interest is not paid on positive balances in this
account. Customers may close the account at any time with 30 days notice,
and any remaining balance is returned by SEPA transfer within 5 business
days.
