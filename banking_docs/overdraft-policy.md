# Meridian Bank - Overdraft Policy (FICTIONAL)

Meridian Bank is a fictional institution created for this study project.

An arranged overdraft of up to 3,000 EUR is available on the Everyday
Current Account after a credit check. The arranged overdraft interest rate
is 9.9% APR, calculated daily and charged monthly. If the account goes
beyond the arranged limit, an unarranged overdraft fee of 15 EUR per month
applies and interest rises to 14.9% APR on the excess amount. Customers
receive an SMS alert when the balance enters the overdraft and again at
80% of the arranged limit. Customers may reduce or cancel the arranged
overdraft at any time in the app; increases require a new credit check.
