# Meridian Bank - Savings Account Interest (FICTIONAL)

Meridian Bank is a fictional institution created for this study project.

The Instant Savings account pays 2.10% AER on balances up to 50,000 EUR and
1.20% AER on the portion above. Interest accrues daily and is credited
monthly. There are no withdrawal limits or notice periods. Deposits are
protected up to 100,000 EUR per customer under the fictional Meridian
deposit protection scheme. New customers receive a 3.00% AER promotional
rate for the first 4 months on balances up to 25,000 EUR. Interest is
taxable income; customers receive an annual interest statement each January.
