# Meridian Bank - Complaints Process (FICTIONAL)

Meridian Bank is a fictional institution created for this study project.

Complaints can be raised in the app, by phone, or in writing. Meridian
acknowledges complaints within 2 business days and aims to resolve them
within 15 business days; complex payment or credit cases may take up to 35
business days, with progress updates at least weekly. If the customer is not
satisfied with the final response, they may escalate to the fictional
Financial Ombudsman of Meridiana within 6 months of the final response
letter. Raising a complaint is free and never affects the customer's
account terms.
